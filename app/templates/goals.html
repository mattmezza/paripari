Goals

A target is a one-off total, not a monthly amount. Progress is your savings, investment and pension balances — checking, buffers, envelopes, gold and property are left out. The projected date assumes your whole monthly surplus goes into each goal, so two goals are not queued against each other. Give a goal a deadline and it gets an on-track verdict; without one, only the projected date is shown.
